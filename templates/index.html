01 WHO THIS IS FOR

The higher the opportunity, the more the conversation is about judgement.

If you're preparing at this level, the problem is rarely knowing the answer. It's making your thinking visible, your ownership unmistakable, and your value obvious without ever sounding rehearsed.

01

The Senior Operator

You've run significant teams and P&Ls. Your career speaks for itself — but the interview doesn't yet reflect the scale of what you've actually owned.

02

The First-Time C-Suite

You're stepping into the room as an equal for the first time. The questions are less about experience and more about how you think, decide, and carry the role.

03

The Career Pivot

You're moving across industries, geographies, or functions. Your story needs to be re-architected for a room that won't interpret your track record on its own terms.

02 THE METHODOLOGY

Four moves that shift how your experience is read.

Every engagement runs through the same underlying framework. Not a script. A way of structuring how your value appears in the room.

01

Establish Ownership

Move past responsibilities and job descriptions. Establish the actual scale of what you've owned — decisions, budgets, people, outcomes.

02

Quantify Impact

Translate activity into business value. Commercial outcomes, organisational shifts, and the specific decisions you drove.

03

Demonstrate Judgement

Show how you think beyond your current remit. Where you see the next layer, and how you'd operate at the level you're interviewing for.

04

Hold the Room

Handle difficult, ambiguous, and senior-level questions with the composure and precision that the role demands.
